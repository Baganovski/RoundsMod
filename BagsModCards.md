# Bag's Mod cards

Cards in the `Bag's Mod` category. Add a row when a card is added, and change the row when that card changes. Names, descriptions, and stat lines are the ones on the card. A stat line with no color note is green. `(red)` is a cost. Stacks is whether `allowMultiple` is true. Effect is what the card does in a fight.

| Card | Rarity | Theme | Description | Stat lines | Stacks | Effect |

| 3D PRINTED BULLETS | Common | TechWhite | Grey plastic bullets | +3 AMMO, -40% DMG (red), -10% Bullet speed (red) | Yes | Adds 3 ammo, multiplies damage by 0.6, and multiplies bullet speed by 0.9. Each spawned bullet, its trail, and its hit puff are painted grey. Another copy stacks those stats again. Removing the last copy leaves later shots yellow. |
| LEE LUCK | Uncommon | DefensiveBlue | Ignore a hit per 10s |  | No | Ignores one bullet every 10 seconds, including a bullet you fired that comes back and hits you. The bullet passes through, so poison and parasite do not start. It is not spent while blocking, dead, or in cease-fire, and a bullet with no damage does not spend it. While it is ready the player glows green, and MISS pops up when a bullet is ignored. |
